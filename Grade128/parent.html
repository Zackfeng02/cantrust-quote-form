How to read this report

This report covers the 12 Ontario expectation groups currently mapped for the learner’s grade. It is a curriculum sample, not the complete Ontario program. An unlisted expectation has not been assessed by MapleQuest. “Not assessed” means there is not yet enough evidence; it does not mean the learner lacks the skill. Use these signals to choose practice and questions for a teacher, not to diagnose a learning difficulty.
