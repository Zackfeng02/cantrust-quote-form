Ontario curriculum sources

MapleQuest is built for Ontario learners in Grades 1–8. Its learning map uses concise, original interpretations of expectations from Ontario curriculum documents.

  • Ontario · Mathematics, Grades 1–8
  • Ontario · Language, Grades 1–8
  • Ontario · Science and Technology, Grades 1–8
  • Ontario · Social Studies, History and Geography

The learning outcomes and questions are concise, original interpretations for demonstration. They are not an official assessment and do not replace a teacher’s judgement.
